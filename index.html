Prepare to dive

Every depth rolls a new terrain mix. Find fuel, open pressure pockets, flood tunnels, and decide how much trouble you can survive.

ONE THUMB IS ENOUGH

Your first dive

  1. Move: drag the joystick with your thumb through open tunnels.
  2. Dig: press and hold any visible tile. The glowing route previews known finds and hazards. Lift your thumb to stop.
  3. Fuel: hard rock spends fuel; fuel also powers the beacon. Its first find is never guaranteed.
  4. Experiment: hold CHARGE and drag to place a blast. Gas can chain; water floods connected tunnels and can reveal habitats.
  5. Extract: the beacon needs one fuel. It saves 20% of your loose cargo. Pressure and hazards can destroy the ship.
