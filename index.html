One thumb is all you needQuick controls tour
  • Move: drag anywhere on the world. Tap a spot to walk there.
  • Gather / Collect: hold the big button, or tap a resource tile. Near stored extractor goods, it becomes Collect.
  • Trail: hold Gather, then draw freely across the world. Green tiles show exactly what the sweep will gather; release to sweep them.
  • Build / Upgrade: open a panel from the dock, choose an option, then tap Done.
  • Zoom: tap − or +. Pinch zoom is optional.
  • Mine: tap MINE to enter below the forest. Move with the same joystick, but only through open tunnels. Use GATHER to tap or trace-dig nearby rock into a path. Each layer has a marked shaft: dig it open, then tap its hatch when you are ready to descend.
